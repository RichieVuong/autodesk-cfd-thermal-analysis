# Electronics Enclosure Thermal Analysis

An Autodesk CFD case study analyzing airflow and heat transfer in a compact electronics enclosure. The goal was to identify hot spots, compare vent layouts, and recommend a design that improves cooling without increasing the enclosure footprint.

## What I did

- Prepared the enclosure geometry for simulation
- Assigned material and thermal properties
- Defined inlet, outlet, and heat-source conditions
- Reviewed airflow velocity and temperature results
- Compared vent layouts and documented the design recommendation

## Result

The analysis showed that vent placement and a clear intake-to-exhaust path were more important than simply increasing the total vent area. The recommended layout reduced recirculation near the warm components and created a more direct cooling path.
